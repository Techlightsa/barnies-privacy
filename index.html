مع من نشاركها (معالجو البيانات)

  • شركة ضوء التقنية (Techlight): تشغّل الخادم الذي يستقبل الطلبات ويحفظ الحساب والأختام نيابةً عنا.
  • Moyasar: بوابة الدفع الإلكتروني (عند اختيارك الدفع أونلاين).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: إيصال الإشعارات إلى جهازك.
  • مزوّد الرسائل النصية لإرسال رمز التحقق إلى جوالك.
  • الفرع الذي تطلب منه: يرى اسمك ورقمك وطلبك وعنوان التوصيل لتجهيز الطلب.

Privacy policy — Barnie's Cafe app

Effective date: 2026-11-17

Payment details: you type your card details (or STC Pay number) into the payment form inside the app and they are sent directly to Moyasar; they never reach us, are not stored in the app and are not used for anything else. We do not use your data for advertising and never sell it.

Who we share it with (processors)

  • Techlight (ضوء التقنية): runs the server that receives orders and keeps your account and stamps on our behalf.
  • Moyasar: online payment gateway (when you choose to pay online).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications to your device.
  • The SMS provider that sends your sign-in code.
  • The branch you order from: it sees your name, number, order and delivery address to prepare the order.

Retention

Account data is kept while the account exists; invoice records for the period Saudi law requires.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account → Delete account. Deletion erases your name, email and addresses, stops sign-in and notifications, and removes your cart and saved data from the device. Issued invoices are kept as the law requires. You can sign up again with the same number. Without the app (for example after uninstalling it): to delete your account, email itech.4prog@gmail.com (or call 0534253038) from or quoting your registered mobile number; we delete it within 30 days and confirm to you. The same data is erased as from the app; issued invoices are kept as the law requires. For any other request about your data write to itech.4prog@gmail.com.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law.
